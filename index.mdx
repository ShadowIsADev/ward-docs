This documentation is for the Ward Systems [Discord bot](https://wardsystems.xyz/). Ward streamlines the security of communities on the Discord platform.

## Key features

Six layers of protection that cover the issues most communities end up dealing with.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Server Protection" icon="shield-halved">
    - **Backups and restores:** bring channels, roles, and permissions back quickly.
    - **Lockdown mode:** stop risky actions when something looks wrong.
    - **Webhook checks:** remove rogue webhooks and obvious spam.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Join Gates and Raids" icon="door-closed">
    - **Fast join detection:** spot bot-like bursts before they pile up.
    - **Verification checks:** add steps for suspicious new joins.
    - **Alt account checks:** flag fresh or risky accounts early.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Community Reports" icon="flag">
    - **Easy reporting:** flag risky accounts without extra friction.
    - **Trusted reporters:** give reliable reports more weight.
    - **Soft isolation:** pause suspicious users before a final review.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Moderation Tools" icon="gavel">
    - **Quick actions:** kick, ban, or mute without digging through menus.
    - **Escalating punishments:** warnings can become mutes and bans.
    - **Proof required:** attach context before actions are finalized.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Staff Workflow" icon="users-gear">
    - **Ticketing:** keep reports organized and easier to follow up on.
    - **Activity tracking:** see when staff are stepping in.
    - **Review queues:** make consistent decisions with context.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Auto-Mod Filters" icon="filter">
    - **Token scanner:** delete exposed bot tokens and keys.
    - **Scam blocking:** stop fake Nitro links and sketchy domains.
    - **Spam filter:** cut off floods and mass mentions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
