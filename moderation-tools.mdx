<Card title="Overview of Community Moderation Tools" icon="brick-wall-shield">
  Ward Systems Community Moderation Tools are designed to help protect, manage, and maintain Discord communities. With a range of powerful moderation features, Ward Systems helps staff respond to incidents, enforce community rules, and maintain a safe and organised environment.

  Ward Systems Moderation tools uses a Scoring system called **Ward Score** 
</Card>

<Card title="Community Moderation Tools" icon="sparkles">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open the dashboard">
      Navigate to [https://dashboard.wardsystems.xyz/](https://wardsystems.xyz/dashboard/).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in with Discord">
      Authorize with your Discord account so Ward can list the servers you manage
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select your server">
      Pick the server you want to configure. You need **Manage Server** permission for it to appear.
    </Step>
    <Step title="**Navigate to the Community Moderation Tool Module to configure and manage your community's moderation tool settings**">
      [https://dashboard.wardsystems.xyz/modules/community-mod-tools](https://wardsystems.xyz/dashboard/module/community-mod-tools)
    </Step>
  </Steps>
