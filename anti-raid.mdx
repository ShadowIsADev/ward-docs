<Card title=" Overview of Anti-Raid Protection " icon="bomb">
  Ward Systems Anti-Raid Protection is designed to help defend Discord communities against coordinated raids and large-scale malicious activity. It monitors for suspicious behaviour and provides moderation teams with tools to quickly detect, respond to, and contain potential raid attempts.
</Card>

<Card title="Anti-Raid Protection Setup" icon="sparkles">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open the dashboard">
      Navigate to [https://dashboard.wardsystems.xyz/](https://wardsystems.xyz/dashboard/).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in with Discord">
      Authorize with your Discord account so Ward can list the servers you manage
    </Step>
    <Step title="**Select your server**">
      Pick the server you want to configure. You need **Manage Server** permission for it to appear.
    </Step>
    <Step title="**Navigate to the Anti-Raid Module to configure and manage your community's raid protection settings**">
      Navigate to the **Anti-Raid Module** to configure and manage your community's raid protection settings:

      [https://dashboard.wardsystems.xyz/modules/anti-raid](https://wardsystems.xyz/modules/anti-raid)
    </Step>
    <Step title="**Test your Anti-Raid Protection configuration**">
      Test your Anti-Raid Protection configuration to ensure the selected security settings are working as expected. Confirm that alerts, automated protections, and other configured actions respond correctly.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Card>
