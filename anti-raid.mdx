<Card title="Overview of Anti-Raid Protection " icon="bomb">
  Ward Systems Anti-Raid Protection is designed to help defend Discord communities against coordinated raids and large-scale malicious activity. It monitors for suspicious behaviour and provides moderation teams with tools to quickly detect, respond to, and contain potential raid attempts.
</Card>

<Card title="Anti-Raid Protection Setup" icon="sparkles">
