<Card title="Overview of Community Reports" icon="shield-alert">
  Ward Systems **Community Reports** provides a structured system for reporting suspicious users, servers, messages, and harmful activity to official Ward Systems staff. Submitted reports are reviewed and verified by Ward staff, with verified reports contributing to Ward's intelligence and **Ward Score** system.

  The Ward Score helps Ward identify potentially suspicious users and can be used across Ward Systems security features, including Join Gate, Anti-Raid Protection, and other server defense systems.
</Card>
